1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your device by your browser to save configuration states or help third-party services track web interactions.

2. How We Use Cookies

Essential Storage (localStorage): We use basic local storage to remember your direct visual settings (e.g., UI dark mode or language preferences). These do not track personal information.

Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics): We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site — for example, which tools are popular and how the site can be improved. These cookies are blocked by default and are only set once you accept them via our cookie banner.

3. Consent and Control

In compliance with European ePrivacy laws, analytics and advertising cookies are blocked by default when you first load this website. They are only enabled if you explicitly give consent via our on-site cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time in the panel below, or clear/disable cookies entirely through your web browser's configuration menu.
