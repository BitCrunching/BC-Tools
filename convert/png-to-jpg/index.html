Why convert a PNG to JPG?

PNG is lossless, which makes it the right call for logos and screenshots — but that same lossless quality means PNG files are often several times larger than they need to be for an ordinary photo, especially once a screenshot has real photographic detail in it.

If you don't need transparency and just want a smaller file for email, a form upload with a size limit, or a faster-loading web page, JPG usually gets you there with no visible difference.

Not sure which format to use?

Here's what each format is actually for. Use "I have this" to set it as your Input, or "I need this" to set it as your Output.

JPG

  • Best for everyday photos.
  • Small files, great compatibility.

PNG

  • Best for logos, screenshots, and transparency.
  • Lossless quality.

WEBP

  • Best for websites.
  • Smaller files with transparency support.

HEIC

  • iPhone's default photo format — accepted as input.
  • Convert it to JPG, PNG, or WEBP for wider compatibility.

PDF

  • Best for sharing multiple images as one document.
  • You can also merge them with our Combine tool.
