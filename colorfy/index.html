New to picking colors from images?

Drop in any image and Colorfy starts you off with two pickers already sampling two different spots — drag either ring anywhere over the image (or just click a spot) to sample somewhere else, and the code below updates live. Hit the + to add up to 4 pickers at once, each with its own row below. Switch between HEX, RGB, HSB, and HSL from the dropdown, and click any code to copy it straight to your clipboard.
