Our story

For what felt like many lightyears, using free web tools has felt like navigating through a black hole — endless ads, bloated interfaces, and painfully slow loading times.

In early 2026, we decided enough was enough and started building a better way: fast, clean, browser-based tools that creators actually enjoy using.

BC Tools is currently being developed by a single alien using AI technology he stole on his last trip through Sector 7G. His mission is simple: create tools that let people focus on creating.

He also brought the BC Tools logo, but he would not tell us what it means. He told us, "Human civilization is not prepared for it yet." He did not want to reveal his identity here, so we will only tell you that he likes the color sky blue.

Word around the office is his home planet, Bonbonia🍬, is famous throughout the galaxy for exactly one thing: candy. We haven't asked him about it directly, but there's a suspicious amount of wrapper litter near his desk.

Our Beliefs

The standard for web tools needs to change.

Great tools should have clean interfaces, load quickly, and respect the creator's time. Always ready to execute, and always here to help your projects come to life.

Everything should be within reach — no worm holes, no subscription traps, just fast and reliable tools that work.

Our goals

Our goal is to build the ultimate browser-based Creative Hub.

Whether you work with images, videos, audio, or code, if a tool can run directly in your browser, we want to bring it together in one place accessible from anywhere.
