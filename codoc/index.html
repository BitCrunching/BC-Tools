New to Codoc?

Codoc turns a Word document into a PDF entirely inside your browser — the file is parsed and rendered on your own device, nothing is uploaded anywhere. It reads modern Word files (.docx, Word 2007 and later); the older binary .doc format isn't supported.

Complex layouts — multi-column text, headers/footers, and precise pagination — may render slightly differently than in Word, since Codoc rebuilds the page flow from the document's content rather than Word's own print engine. For straightforward documents (headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, images) the result matches closely.
