## v26.10.5

## New tool: Comprio

* Comprio shrinks a video's file size right in your browser (MP4, MOV, WebM and more). Nothing is uploaded.
* Pick Low, Medium or High and every video shows its estimated new size and percentage before you compress. Several videos at once each get their own estimate.
* Click the resolution on a video's preview to scale that video down (the shorter side is used, so portrait, 4K, 8K and ultrawide videos keep their proportions). The original resolution is marked with an asterisk.
* A small "i" appears on a video when it is already heavily compressed (the real result can be larger than estimated) or when it can't be saved as MP4 (8K, odd sizes), in which case it is saved as a WebM file at the exact same size.
* Cancel or remove a batch at any time. Unreadable or non-video files are reported without stopping the rest.
* Built for big jobs: tested with 4K and 8K clips, 10-minute videos, and batches of up to 50 files.
* New logo, a homepage card with a Read more preview, a Golden Rules guide with five articles, and a Comprio link in the homepage footer.
* Translated into all 12 languages: the tool, the homepage card, the Golden Rules guide and the sitemap.
