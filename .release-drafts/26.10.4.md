## v26.10.4

## New tool: Colorfy

* Colorfy is back, rebuilt on the current design: drop an image, drag the picker to any pixel and get its exact HEX, RGB, HSB or HSL code. Up to 4 pickers at once, and a saved-colors palette of up to 20 that you can name.
* New logo, a homepage card with a Read more preview, and a Golden Rules guide with five articles.
* Translated into all 12 languages.

## Report button: bugs, translations and feedback

* The report popup now has three tabs: **Bug**, **Translation** and **Feedback**. Each goes to its own channel on our Discord server.
* Translation and feedback let you tell us which wording is wrong or share an idea, with up to 3 screenshots on any tab.
* The consent text now says plainly that a report is not anonymous. After sending, the popup shows "Thank you for your help!" in a terminal-style line.
* Privacy, Terms and Cookies were updated to match, in all languages.
* The popup is translated into all 12 languages and has a Close button on phones.

## Header

* The report, language and share panels open on hover on desktop. Moving from one to another swaps them, and moving the mouse away closes the panel (your unsent text is kept).
* On phones the language menu opens at the right edge of the screen.
* Flags have a thin outline in the theme color, so white flags stay visible in the light theme.

## Congify

* Result area: the status line ("Encoding GIF...") sits right under the Convert button, and the Result row with Download is below the result frame.

## Translations

* In every language, the help banner no longer overflows: the title sits above Back / Close / Next on phones, and the buttons stay on one line.
* The "Single files / .zip" toggle and the color pickers no longer cut off longer labels.

## Search results

* New page title and description for the home page ("BC Tools - NEW STANDARD"), in every language.
