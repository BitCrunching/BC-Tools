## v26.10.2

## New look for buttons, menus and panels

* All tools now share one button style: a theme-colored outline at rest that fills in on hover, in both light and dark themes.
* Dropdowns, combo menus, popups, toggles and the nav buttons use the same style, with 1px borders throughout.
* Banners and panels (file cards, terminal-style help banners, the homepage "next tool" box) use their own softer #636363 outline, so they read apart from the buttons.
* The red remove-all button and the mobile back arrow were restyled to match.

## Across all tools

* Esc closes any open popup or menu, and opening one closes the others.
* Pressing D on the intro screen continues where you left off.
* Dropdown option rows have a fixed height, and the selected option no longer shows a check mark.
* Codify: Mac nav and Shadow use ON/OFF buttons.

## Convert

* Converted files can be downloaded again as many times as you like.

## Bugs fixed

* Codify: copying or saving the image could fail or flicker. It now renders from an off-screen copy.
* Context and Congify: several mobile layout fixes.
* Result cards have a fixed width, and long file names stay on one line with an ellipsis.
