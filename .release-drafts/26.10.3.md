## v26.10.3

## 12 languages

* BC Tools is now available in Czech, German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese and Romanian. Pick yours with the flag button in the header; your choice is remembered.
* Tool names stay in English everywhere.
* The translations are machine-written and not yet native-reviewed, so wording feedback is welcome.

## Report a bug

* New 🐛 button in the header and footer: describe the problem in up to 100 characters, attach up to 3 screenshots and press Send (or Enter).
* Reports go to our Discord server through a small relay, with the page, screen and browser details. A consent checkbox is required before sending.
* The relay only accepts requests from bitcrunching.com, checks that attachments are real images and rate-limits abuse.
* Privacy, Terms and Cookies were restructured around the on-device guarantee, which does not apply to this button.

## Context

* Text, signatures and shapes now export at the exact on-screen position, including on rotated PDF pages.
* Each signature is embedded once and downscaled to its placed size, saving about 200 KB per signature.
* A "File size" line under the editor shows the current PDF size.
* The red remove button sits beside the text box, opposite the move handle.

## Shortcuts and help

* (A) adds files: the Add tile, the Add pill and the intro drop zone all respond to it, and every tool's help banner now mentions it.
* The FAQ has an entry on keyboard shortcuts.

## Header and buttons

* New curved-arrow share icon; the language and share menus open on hover.
* The header logo is 40x40 up to 768px wide, and "BC Tools" is hidden at 415px and below.
* Color buttons are locked at 107x40 in every tool.
* Result cards and previews follow the shared button border; banners and panels keep their own #636363 outline.
* The share-with-a-friend banner was removed.

## Other

* Congify shows the GIF size in the done status.
* About: the Bonbonia candy wobbles on hover again.
* All 16 Convert route pages were regenerated from the current Convert page, fixing missing buttons.
* Privacy and Terms wording updated, including a built-in scanner note and "sell" in the data promise.
