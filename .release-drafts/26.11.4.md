## v26.11.4

## New tool: Cqualy

* Cqualy is a free equalizer that runs in your browser. Load a song or the sound of a video, move the sliders, and the sound wave updates live so you see what you are changing.
* Choose 7, 11, 15 or 31 bands, start from one of 14 presets, and fine-tune from there. Volume sits beside the wave; Balance and Speed sit below the sliders.
* Trim the start and end by dragging the handles on the wave. Playback and the download stay inside your selection.
* Download as MP3 or WAV in Mono, Stereo, 5.1 or 7.1. Moving Balance on a mono file switches the output to Stereo so the panning is heard.
* Click any dB value to see every control in percent, click the time to switch between elapsed and remaining, and click a label to reset it.
* "Continue where you left off" brings your file and settings back after a refresh. Shortcuts: Space and arrows to play and seek, plus D, R, C, U, P, B, T. The help banner lists them.
* On a phone held upright Cqualy asks you to turn the phone sideways, the same way Codify does.
* Available in all 12 languages, with its own FAQ answers and guide. A Cqualy link is in every page footer.

* Cqualy audio engine is `window.cqEngine` in `cqualy/cqualy-tool.js`: biquad chain, coalesced `OfflineAudioContext` re-render for the live wave, trim range, and a WAV extensible writer for more than 2 channels.
